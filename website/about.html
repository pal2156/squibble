About Squibble

What Squibble is

Squibble is a free, open-source tool that automates academic research outreach for students. You tell it which university you are targeting and what you want to research. It finds the relevant faculty, ranks them by fit against your profile, drafts a personalized email for each one, and sends them from your own email account. When a professor replies, you see it instantly in your dashboard.

The core idea is simple: students should not have to do hours of work before knowing if anyone will respond. Squibble handles the outreach upfront so you only invest real effort after a professor replies.

Squibble is self-hosted: you run it on your own computer, bring your own AI provider key, and connect your own email. There's no account to create and no Squibble server in the middle — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what that means.

The founder

Paul Lacarriere is a student studying Mathematics and Physics at Columbia University. When he was looking for a research position, he tried doing the outreach manually and found it took far too long. He designed a structured workflow to automate the process, ran it himself, and found his research position in a single day. Squibble is that workflow, released as free, open-source software so anyone can run it themselves.

Questions or feedback? Reach us at contact@squibble.pro or open an issue on GitHub.

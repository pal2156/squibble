1. The software

Squibble is a self-hosted tool that helps students find and contact academic researchers. It uses AI (via a provider you configure) to discover relevant faculty profiles, draft personalized outreach emails based on your academic profile, and send those emails from your own email account. It also monitors your inbox for replies and classifies them automatically. You run it on your own machine, and you remain fully responsible for all emails sent through your own account.

Important: Professor information (including lab focus, contact details, and research availability) is sourced from publicly available faculty pages and may not be current or accurate. AI-generated emails are drafts and should be reviewed by you before sending. Squibble does not warrant the accuracy of any faculty profile data.

Quick start

Node.js 20+ and an AI provider key is all you need.

# clone and install git clone https://github.com/pal2156/squibble cd squibble npm install # run it npm run dev

Open localhost:3000 and the setup wizard walks you through connecting an AI provider and your email account.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Yes. Squibble is open source and self-hosted — there's no subscription and no credit system. You bring your own AI provider key (Gemini has a genuine free tier) and your own email account, and run it on your own computer.
Squibble uses AI to search the faculty pages of any university and rank professors by how well they match your research interests and background.
Yes. Every email is written using your specific profile, your coursework, your experience, and the professor's actual research. No two emails are the same.
Squibble polls your own inbox over IMAP in the background, detects replies from professors you contacted, and classifies each one as positive or negative. You see it show up in your dashboard automatically.
Squibble runs entirely on your own machine — there's no Squibble server collecting your profile, emails, or replies. The only third parties involved are the AI provider and email host you personally configure. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Both. Squibble works for any type of research position, paid or unpaid.
Node.js 20+, an API key from an AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini), and an email account that supports app passwords (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, or similar). No credit card, no OAuth app registration, no cloud database.

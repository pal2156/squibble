Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Squibble ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Paul Lacarriere, handles data related to squibble.pro (this website) and the free, open-source Squibble software you download and run yourself.

1. The short version

Squibble is self-hosted software. When you download and run it, it runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Squibble account, no Squibble server, and no Squibble database that your profile, campaigns, emails, or replies pass through. We do not have access to any of it, because there is no path for it to reach us.

2. This website (squibble.pro)

squibble.pro is a static, informational website — it explains what Squibble is and links to the source code. It does not have a login, does not set tracking cookies, and does not run analytics. The only data it can receive from you is whatever you choose to send by emailing contact@squibble.pro.

3. Data the Squibble software stores locally

When you run Squibble on your own machine, it stores the following locally, on your own disk, and nowhere else:

  • Profile information. Your school, academic year, major, coursework, research experience, skills, and any resume you upload — in a local SQLite database file.
  • Campaign data. The research interests, universities, and position types you search for.
  • Professor and email data. The faculty shortlist Squibble finds, the emails it drafts and sends, and the replies it detects.
  • Credentials. Your AI provider API key and email (SMTP/IMAP) credentials, written to a local .env.local file in the project folder on your machine.

None of this is transmitted to us. There is no sync, no telemetry, and no remote backup built into the software.

4. Third parties you choose to connect

Squibble's only network calls go to the services you personally configure during setup:

  • Your AI provider. Whichever of Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI, or Gemini (Google) you choose. Your profile, campaign parameters, faculty research summaries, and drafted emails are sent to that provider's API using your own API key, governed by that provider's privacy policy — not ours.
  • Your email provider. Squibble sends outreach emails via SMTP and checks for replies via IMAP, using the email account and app password you provide during setup. Only messages related to professors you contacted through Squibble are read.

We are not a party to any of these connections. We never see your API keys, your email credentials, or the content that flows between your machine and these providers.

5. Source code and issue tracking

Squibble's source code is hosted publicly on GitHub. If you open an issue, pull request, or discussion there, that activity is subject to GitHub's privacy policy, not this one.

6. Children's privacy

Squibble is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the software runs entirely on hardware you control and we operate no accounts, we have no way of collecting information about who runs it. If you have concerns about a child's use of the software, that's a matter of local device and account controls on your end.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the software's data model changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date and, where relevant, noted in the GitHub repository.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email contact@squibble.pro or open an issue on GitHub.
